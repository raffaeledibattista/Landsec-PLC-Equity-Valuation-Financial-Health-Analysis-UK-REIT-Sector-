# Landsec PLC: Equity Valuation & Financial Health Analysis

## Project Overview
Comprehensive equity research report and financial valuation of **Land Securities Group PLC (FTSE 100)**, developed during the Real Estate Finance & Investment module at Regent's University London.

The project evaluates Landsec's operational resilience, balance sheet deleveraging, and asset rotation across its £10.9bn multi-asset portfolio.

## Key Findings & Methodology
* **Capital Structure & Covenants:** Analyzed LTV ratios, Net Debt/EBITDA trajectory, and Tier 1 debt covenant headroom (ICR ≥ 1.85x).
* **Valuation Framework (5-Year Forecast):**
  * **Two-Stage Dividend Discount Model (DDM):** Built around REIT payout dynamics, lease reversion gaps, and a 10.03% CAPM Cost of Equity ($K_e$).
  * **Peer Multiples Benchmarking:** Compared Price/EPRA Earnings and EV/EBITDA against British Land and Derwent London.
* **Investment Conclusion:** Derived an intrinsic fair value of **672.16p per share**, highlighting an **18% discount (margin of safety)** against current market levels and resulting in a **Buy** recommendation.
